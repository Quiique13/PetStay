1. **Subject and instructions.** The processor provides account-scoped hosting,
   synchronization, display, export, recovery and deletion of hotel records only
   on the controller's documented lawful instructions, including instructions
   concerning transfers. The service agreement, this agreement and authorized
   support requests define those instructions. Where law requires different
   processing, the processor informs the controller before processing unless
   prohibited by law. The processor informs the controller if an instruction
   appears to infringe applicable data-protection law.
2. **Confidentiality and security.** Authorized personnel are bound by
   confidentiality and receive access limited to their duties. The processor
   implements appropriate technical and organizational measures under Article 32
   as completed in Schedule B. Security descriptions must reflect actual controls.
3. **Subprocessors.** Schedule C identifies approved subprocessors, purposes,
   locations and transfers. The parties select [specific written authorization /
   general written authorization with advance notice and an objection procedure]
   and complete notice timing, objection grounds and resolution. Equivalent
   applicable data-protection duties must bind each subprocessor; the processor
   remains responsible for its subprocessor obligations under Article 28.
4. **Assistance.** Taking account of the processing, the processor assists the
   controller with rights requests, security, breach notification, impact
   assessments and prior consultation. Requests from data subjects concerning
   hotel records are forwarded without undue delay and not independently
   answered except on instructions or as legally required.
5. **Incidents.** The processor informs the controller without undue delay after
   becoming aware of a personal-data breach, supplies available relevant facts,
   updates as more becomes known, and supports containment and investigation.
   The controller determines its authority/data-subject notification duties.
6. **End of service.** At the controller's choice, return or delete personal data
   and delete existing copies after service ends unless legally required to
   retain them. Complete [export format, transition period, backup expiry,
   retained categories/legal basis, confirmation method] in Schedule A.
7. **Accountability and audits.** The processor makes information necessary to
   demonstrate Article 28 compliance available, and allows and contributes to
   audits and inspections by the controller or its mandated auditor. Agree
   practical security and confidentiality arrangements without defeating that
   right. Inform the controller of unlawful instructions.
8. **Transfers.** No transfer outside the EEA occurs without documented
   instructions and a valid Chapter V mechanism. Schedule C must identify the
   mechanism and any necessary supplementary measures and supporting assessment.
9. **Other terms.** This agreement governs processing duties; commercial terms
   must not contradict it or reduce mandatory legal duties. Identify governing
   law, contacts, authorized instruction channels and signature method.

## Schedule A — processing description (complete and approve)

- Subject: hosted animal-hotel management records.
- Nature: storage, retrieval, modification, synchronization, export and erasure.
- Purpose: managing guests/tutors, stays, prices and payment status for the hotel.
- People: tutors/customers and any identifiable people in uploaded content.
- Data: names, hotel/customer references, linked pet details/photos, stay dates,
  prices and payment status. No special-category human data is intended.
- Duration, retention/deletion periods and backup handling: [complete].
- Controller rights/obligations: lawful instructions, necessary notices and legal
  basis, minimization, accuracy and handling of data-subject rights.

## Schedule B — actual security measures (verify, do not assume)

Document authentication, account isolation and tested rules; transport security;
administrative MFA and least privilege; key management; incident handling;
backup/recovery testing; device/cache risks; personnel confidentiality;
vulnerability updates; access review; deletion and audit evidence. Identify
owner and evidence for each. Local device copies are not encrypted per user.
